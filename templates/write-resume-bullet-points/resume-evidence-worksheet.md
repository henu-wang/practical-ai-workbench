## Check before adding to a resume

- [ ] Every number, tool, audience, and scope word comes from the facts.
- [ ] Ownership words match my individual contribution.
- [ ] No outcome or percentage was inferred from an ordinary duty.
- [ ] Unknown facts remain questions, not confident wording.
- [ ] Rewrite is at most 30 whitespace-separated words for this editing exercise.
- [ ] Shortening has not changed the meaning.
- [ ] Final PDF preserves the checked bullet under the correct role.
