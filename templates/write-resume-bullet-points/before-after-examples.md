# Four fictional resume edits

Original manually written examples. None is a real applicant's history or generated model output.
All rewrites are under 30 whitespace-separated words; the examples are not ATS tests.

## 1. Volunteer administration

Before: Handled scheduling and stuff.

Verified facts:
- V1: The volunteer maintained a shared calendar for six tutoring volunteers.
- V2: The volunteer sent session reminders.
- V3: The volunteer checked bookings twice a week.
- Unknown: Attendance improvement, cancellation rate, and money saved.

After: Coordinated a shared calendar for six tutoring volunteers, sent session reminders, and checked bookings twice a week.

Check: V1–V3 support the scope and frequency. The rewrite claims no improvement or saving.

## 2. Customer support with a qualitative result

Before: Helped with customer emails.

Verified facts:
- C1: The employee drafted an FAQ reply guide from the existing team FAQ.
- C2: The supervisor reviewed and approved that guide, and the team adopted it.
- C3: The employee escalated questions outside the guide's answers to the supervisor.
- Unknown: Volume, response time, satisfaction change, and resolution rate.

After: Drafted an FAQ reply guide adopted by the team after supervisor review, and escalated customer questions outside its answers.

Check: C1–C3 support the actions and the qualitative result of adoption. There is no numerical claim, measured improvement, or invented customer outcome.
