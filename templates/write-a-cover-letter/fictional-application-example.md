Employer: Northbank Community Centre
Role: Front Desk Assistant

The Front Desk Assistant answers visitor questions about published activities, maintains room-booking information and hands unresolved requests to the centre coordinator. Clear explanations and accurate booking updates are required. Experience with RoomBook is preferred. Send a resume and a cover letter of no more than 500 words. Address the letter to the Hiring Team.

## Complete letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Front Desk Assistant role at Northbank Community Centre. Through volunteering with a weekend tutoring programme, I have experience answering visitor questions, keeping booking information current and passing unresolved requests to a coordinator. Your posting describes these as part of the role, and they are the responsibilities I would like to build on.

At the tutoring programme, I explained the published timetable and sign-up process to visitors. When a question fell outside the published guidance, I recorded it for the coordinator instead of giving an answer I could not confirm. This experience is relevant to your request for clear visitor information and careful handovers.

I also updated a shared Google Sheets booking list and sent session reminders. My responsibility was to keep that list current, not to manage a commercial booking system. I have not used RoomBook, which your posting lists as preferred, and would be willing to learn it. I can offer the booking-list experience described above without claiming familiarity with software I have not used.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how this experience could support your front desk. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
Alex Morgan
