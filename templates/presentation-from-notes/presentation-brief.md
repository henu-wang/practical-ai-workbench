## Outline acceptance checks

- One message per slide; supporting detail may go in speaker notes.
- Every factual claim has an N-reference.
- Proposals are not presented as existing results.
- Missing names, dates, numbers, or approvals stay unresolved.
- No invented statistics, customer quotes, or business impact.
