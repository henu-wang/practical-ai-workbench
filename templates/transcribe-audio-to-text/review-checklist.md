## Acceptance checks

- [ ] Opening and ending agree with the recording.
- [ ] Proper names, numbers, dates, and commitments were checked by listening.
- [ ] Silence or unclear speech has not become invented content.
- [ ] Uncertain passages include an audio time and uncertainty label.
- [ ] Speaker labels are supported, or explicitly unknown.
- [ ] Edits that remove repetition are labeled as an edited copy.
- [ ] Original recognition output remains available separately.
- [ ] Remaining uncertainties are listed before the transcript is shared.
