# Audience and demand contract

## Audience

English-speaking people with a concrete task who can benefit from TokRepo assets: knowledge workers, freelancers, creators, analysts, and small-business operators. “Broader demand” means everyday task intent, not unsupported claims of high volume. Technical names may appear where the task genuinely requires them, but should not automatically drive topic selection.

Record country and language separately. Start research with US English; add the UK, Canada, Australia, or other markets only when evidence is available. A US signal does not establish global English demand.

## Required demand packet

Record the exact query, observed intent, source URL, observation time, market/language, observed signal, limitations, and why the TokRepo combination can serve it.

A qualified topic requires either a positive quantitative search signal with its exact metric/period/geography, or a matching search suggestion/related search plus at least two relevant recurring public questions from independent sources. Current searches must be inspected; do not infer demand from an invented title or a repository list.

Autocomplete is qualitative. Existing search results are supply, old question views are historical attention, and GSC impressions are observed visibility for this property. None is a monthly-volume estimate. An inaccessible keyword field remains unknown. No promised traffic, ranking, or indexing speed.

Compare intent and alternatives: template seekers need a template; installation seekers need verified supported setup; people wanting immediate conversion should not be baited into an unrelated AI asset. Reject popular topics when the asset combination cannot deliver a credible result.

## Canonical intent ownership

Read the complete intent ledger before choosing topics. One page owns a task intent, defined by reader, input, desired output, and workflow. Synonyms generally map to one page. Update an existing page when it already serves the intent; a renamed keyword or different introduction is not a new topic.

Each entry records `intent_id`, primary/related queries, task, pillar, primary/secondary asset IDs and roles, canonical path, evidence packet, publication date, review source hash, and status. A new page needs a materially different requested output or workflow, not a different keyword spelling.

At most six qualified new canonical intents per Beijing date across all runs. Initial corrected revisions to existing pages consume no new-intent quota, and do not justify publishing additional pages to fill an imagined shortfall.

Use measured funnel observations in `METRICS.md` for later topic updates. A successful publish is not a successful demand experiment; an empty analytics response is not proof of zero demand.
