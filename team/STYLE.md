# English editorial style

Lead with a direct answer to the actual search query, then explain the asset combination and useful result. Use everyday English, concrete inputs and expected outputs. Put the primary TokRepo asset link near the beginning; add relevant links where each asset enters the workflow and at the end. Explain what the reader gets on TokRepo without implying every third-party service is free or needs no account.

Each guide needs an original example or downloadable editable template, practical steps, result checks, meaningful limitations and targeted questions. Guides must satisfy the task on the page and then offer reusable assets; avoid forced gates or thin lists of backlinks. Do not copy whole upstream prompts or license-restricted materials. Mark synthetic examples, model-dependent steps and version-sensitive API snippets. Do not claim unrun tests. Keep the UI focused on completing the reader's task, without SEO or operational jargon.
